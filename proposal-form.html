TA SESSION 1 · YOUR PROJECT

A topic you can explain

Three short answers to begin your engineering or research project.

A rough draft is enough today. Name a candidate simulator and say honestly what you still need to find out.

This form runs in your browser. It does not submit your answers to the course staff. Download your draft, save it in your project, and show it to a TA.

1. The problem and goal

What problem matters? What should improve, and how will you measure it? What limits must be respected?

2. The simulator

Which program or model could compute the result? What inputs and outputs does it have? Can it run on your laptop and accept text inputs? A candidate or an open question is fine.

3. The agent's job

What may the agent decide or change? Why might it need reasoning? If you remove the LLM, could a simple search do just as well? Include one thing you still need to find out.

After downloading, move the file to my-project/docs/proposal-draft.md. Keep any previous answers before replacing a file. Save your work in Git using the commands in the repository README. Browser drafts are stored only on this browser/device when you choose “Save draft”.
